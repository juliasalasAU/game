Round 1/20 · You 0

A low-score quiz for a roomful of players

Twenty categories per game, drawn from a pool of 39. Each has many correct answers, and every correct answer carries a score from 100 down to 0 for how many people out of a hundred would have thought of it. Everyone answers each round; the lowest total after twenty rounds wins.

  • 1Each round, every player gives one correct answer. Answers stay hidden until the host reveals.
  • 2Your answer's score is added to your total. A wrong answer costs 100.
  • 3An answer scoring 0 is a rare answer and takes 5 off your total. If you give a correct answer that isn't on the board, the host can accept it, and it counts as a rare answer.

Host a game

Join a game

Play on this phone
