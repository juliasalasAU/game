Rare Answer Twenty

Round 1/20 · You 0

A low-score quiz for a roomful of players

The game from Pointless and Jo færre jo bedre: a category with many correct answers, and the winner is whoever names the one the fewest people thought of.

  • 1Every category comes from the TV show, with the numbers from its survey of 100 people. You give one answer and try to pick the one the fewest people said.
  • 2Your answer's score is added to your total; lowest total wins. An answer nobody in the survey gave is a rare answer and takes 5 off.
  • 3A wrong answer costs 100. If a correct answer isn't on the list, the host can accept it, and it counts as a rare answer.

Host a game

You get a QR code and a four-letter code. Players scan it with their phone camera and join on their own phones.

Join a game
