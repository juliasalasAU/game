Rare Answer Twenty

Round 1/20 · You 0

A low-score quiz for a roomful of players

Every round has a category with many correct answers. Each answer scores by how many people out of a hundred gave it. Lowest total wins; a correct answer nobody gave is a rare answer and takes 5 off.

  • 1Room survey (the usual way to play): first everyone gets 60 seconds to list every answer they can. That builds the board, exactly like the show's survey of 100 people, except the people are you. Then everyone gives one final answer, scored by how many players listed it.
  • 2Surveyed boards: categories where 100 people really were asked (the BBC show in 2012 and a 100-person survey). Just one answer per player, scored by the real numbers.
  • 3A wrong answer costs 100. If a correct answer isn't on the list, the host can accept it, and it counts as a rare answer.
