Survey to Board

Turn the answers from your Google Form into a scored category for Rare Answer Twenty. Everything happens in this page; nothing is uploaded anywhere.

1. Paste the responses

  1. In Google Forms, open Responses and click the Sheets icon to open the responses spreadsheet.
  2. Click the column letter above a question to select the whole column, copy it, and paste it below. (You can also paste the whole sheet; you'll pick the question next.)

2. Tidy up

Answers that look like the same thing with a different spelling are grouped automatically; check the groups. Select two rows and press Merge to combine them. Press Wrong on anything that isn't a valid answer (it stops counting). Rename a row to the spelling you want shown.
